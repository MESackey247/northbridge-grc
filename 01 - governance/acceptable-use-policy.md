# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose
Defines acceptable standards for utilizing company laptops, networks, and cloud infrastructure.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all staff operating Northbridge hardware or accessing company systems remotely.

## 3. Policy Statements
* **Device Protection:** Company devices must have full-disk encryption enabled and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
* **Data Protection:** Patient data must never be downloaded to personal devices or stored on unapproved cloud storage.
* **Phishing & Security Training:** Staff must complete annual security awareness training and report suspicious emails immediately.
