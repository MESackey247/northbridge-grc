# AI & Machine Learning Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose
Establishes security guidelines for using internal ML features (Patient Triage) and external Generative AI tools[cite: 1].

## 2. Scope
Applies to all software engineers, data scientists, and staff interacting with AI/ML tools at Northbridge Health Ltd[cite: 1].

## 3. Policy Statements
* **Prohibition of PII in External AI:** Staff are strictly prohibited from entering patient scheduling data or PII into public AI models (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude).
* **ML Model Governance:** The internal ML triage algorithm must undergo quarterly bias testing and clinical review prior to deployment[cite: 1].
* **Vendor Verification:** Any third-party AI tool must undergo a TPRM security evaluation before adoption[cite: 1].
