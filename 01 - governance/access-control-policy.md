# Access Control Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines mandatory requirements for accessing Northbridge Health Ltd systems and patient scheduling data.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party personnel accessing AWS cloud resources, production databases, and endpoints.

## 3. Policy Statements
* **Principle of Least Privilege:** Users shall only be granted the minimum system access necessary to perform their role.
* **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** MFA is mandatory for all employee accounts, remote access, and AWS IAM access.
* **Access Reviews:** User access permissions shall be formally reviewed by line managers on a quarterly basis.
* **Password Standards:** Passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters and stored using an approved password manager.
