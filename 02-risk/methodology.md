# Information Security Risk Assessment Methodology

## 1. Purpose & Framework Alignment
This document defines the risk assessment process for **Northbridge Health Ltd**. This methodology aligns with **NIST SP 800-30** (*Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments*) and supports **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 6.1.2**.

## 2. Risk Rating System (5x5 Matrix)
Risk scores are calculated using the formula:
**Risk Score = Likelihood (1-5) x Impact (1-5)**

### Likelihood Rating Scale
1. **Very Low:** Unlikely to occur; requires extreme circumstances.
2. **Low:** Possible, but unlikely within the next 12 months.
3. **Moderate:** Could occur within the next 12 months.
4. **High:** Likely to occur multiple times per year.
5. **Very High:** Almost certain to occur frequently.

### Impact Rating Scale
1. **Very Low:** Negligible operational or financial impact.
2. **Low:** Minor impact; no regulatory or patient data compromise.
3. **Moderate:** Moderate operational disruption; minor UK GDPR reporting required.
4. **High:** Severe disruption; exposure of non-sensitive patient PII; financial penalties.
5. **Very High:** Critical failure; compromise of Special Category health data; severe legal/regulatory enforcement from the ICO.

### Risk Thresholds & Actions
* **1–6 (Low):** Acceptable risk. Monitor periodically.
* **8–12 (Medium):** Treat within 60 days. Risk owner assigned.
* **15–25 (High/Critical):** Immediate remediation required within 14 days. Senior leadership escalation.

## 3. Risk Treatment Options
1. **Mitigate:** Apply technical or administrative controls to reduce likelihood or impact.
2. **Accept:** Acknowledge the risk where treatment cost exceeds potential impact.
3. **Transfer:** Shift financial or operational risk to a third party (e.g., cyber insurance).
4. **Avoid:** Eliminate the activity or technology causing the risk.
